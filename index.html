Our Story

A family tradition,
bottled fresh every day.

Maa Khodiyar Dairy was founded in 1998 by our family with a simple belief: every family deserves milk that is as pure and nourishing as what comes straight from a healthy cow. What started as a small operation with two cows has grown into a trusted neighbourhood dairy, but the values have never changed.

We name our dairy after Maa Khodiyar — the mother goddess who symbolises care, protection, and abundance — because that is what we aspire to bring into every home: nourishment that feels like a mother's love.

Every drop of milk, every block of paneer, every jar of ghee carries 25 years of dedication, early mornings, and honest work. We know your family by name, and we take that responsibility seriously.

— The Khodiyar Dairy Family
